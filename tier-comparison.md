## 11. Experience Summary

| | B2C Free | AI Pro | B2B Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| **User opens the app and sees...** | Their people. Cards with notes. | "Today, 3 people need you." Sorted by AI urgency. | "2 Hot leads. 1 expiring quote. 5 follow-ups." Pipeline dashboard. |
| **The app proactively says...** | Nothing. App waits for user to open it. | "👋 Sarah might appreciate a check-in" (push, gentle) | "⚠️ Johnson account: policy renews in 14 days" (push, urgent) |
| **Feeling after 30 days** | "I'm more connected than before." | "Echo knows my relationships better than I do." | "I haven't dropped a single client." |
| **What makes them upgrade** | — | Manual management becomes tedious. 30-day free trial of AI ends. | Client count grows past 50. Too many to track manually. Need deal stages. |
| **What makes them stay** | It works. It's free. It's beautiful. | Relationship memory lives here. Leaving = losing your social memory. | Revenue depends on it. Churn = lost deals. |

---

> **Design principle across all tiers:** Each upgrade doesn't add complexity — it removes work. B2C Free = you do the thinking. AI Pro = the AI surfaces what you'd miss. B2B Pro = the AI manages what would otherwise cost you money.
