# Echo — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [Date]**

## Our Commitment

Echo is designed with privacy as a foundation, not an afterthought. Your relationship data is among the most personal information you have. We treat it accordingly.

---

## 1. What Data Echo Collects

### On-Device Only (Never Leaves Your Phone)
- Your contacts list (names, phone numbers, email addresses, photos)
- Your interaction history (call/message/email timestamps)
- Your notes about contacts
- Your calendar events (if you choose to connect)

### Sent to AI Services (Only with Your Explicit Permission)

When you use AI Pro or Business Pro features, **anonymized, minimal data** is sent to our AI partners:

| Data Sent | What We Anonymize |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Note content for smart openers | Names replaced with "Person A", companies with "Company X" |
| Photos for card/document scanning | Only the image — no metadata, no location |
| Voice recordings for transcription | Audio only — no device ID, no contact info |
| Conversation context for sales coaching | All personal identifiers stripped |

**What is NEVER sent:** Your raw contacts database, your phone number, your Apple ID, your location, your device identifier.

---

## 2. AI Partners

### DeepSeek (Text + Vision AI)
- Purpose: Smart openers, card scanning, document OCR, relationship analysis
- Data retained by DeepSeek: **Zero retention.** API calls are processed and discarded.
- Privacy policy: https://platform.deepseek.com/privacy

### Volcano Engine / ByteDance (Speech AI)
- Purpose: Voice-to-text transcription, text-to-speech for daily briefings
- Data retained: **Zero retention.** Audio is processed and discarded.
- Privacy policy: https://www.volcengine.com/docs/6359/68816

---

## 3. Data Storage

All Echo data is stored **exclusively on your device** using Apple's SwiftData framework. If you enable iCloud sync (optional), data is encrypted end-to-end via your iCloud account. We have no servers. We cannot access your data.

---

## 4. Data You Control

You can, at any time:
- **Revoke AI access** in Settings → toggle off AI features
- **Delete all contacts** from Echo (your device contacts are never modified)
- **Export your data** as PDF or CSV
- **Delete your account** — since all data is local, deleting the app removes everything

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Echo is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes via an in-app notice and update the date above.
