## 描述（App Store Description）

```
Echo is your personal AI relationship assistant. Import your contacts in one tap, and Echo helps you stay connected to the people who matter — friends, family, clients, and colleagues.

▸ WHY ECHO?

Most of us have 500+ contacts but only truly stay in touch with a handful. Social media keeps us "connected" but not actually connected. Echo changes that.

▸ HOW IT WORKS

1. Import your contacts in one tap
2. Echo organizes them into your Echo Layer — the people you genuinely care about
3. See at a glance when you last talked, what you talked about, and who needs your attention
4. Reach out with one tap — call, message, or email directly from the app
5. AI (Pro) analyzes your interaction patterns, suggests what to say, and reminds you when it's been too long

▸ THREE MODES. ONE APP.

• PERSONAL (Free) — Full-featured contact relationship manager. Unlimited contacts, Echo Layer, notes, and one-tap reach.

• AI PRO ($3.99/mo) — DeepSeek-powered AI. Smart openers, auto-layering, rhythm detection, voice notes, and daily insights. 30-day free trial.

• BUSINESS ($14.99/mo) — Your mobile CRM. Kanban deal pipeline, business card scanning, sales coaching, policy/document OCR, CSV export, and team sharing.

▸ PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Your contacts never leave your device. AI features send only anonymized, minimal data to our secure AI partners (DeepSeek + Volcano Engine). You control what gets shared. Always.

▸ PERFECT FOR

• Staying in touch with family and close friends
• Remembering details about people you care about
• Insurance agents managing client renewals
• Real estate agents tracking deals and follow-ups
• Financial advisors maintaining client relationships
• Anyone who has ever said "I should call them more often"

▸ WHAT USERS SAY

"A reminder to call my mom disguised as a beautiful app."
"Finally, a CRM that doesn't feel like work."
"I haven't missed a single client follow-up since I started using Echo."

Download Echo and hear the people you miss.

```
